01 / THE THROUGH-LINECuriosity, made useful.

A quality mindset. A maker’s energy.

I care about what happens after the idea.

Does it work? Can people trust it? Can it grow without getting in its own way?

For more than 16 years, I’ve worked across software quality, test automation and pragmatic engineering. I started close to the details, then moved toward designing systems and approaches that help teams deliver with confidence.

Alongside that work, I founded AJ&W Consulting Limited, known as Kounga Solutions, and began building products of my own. That mix keeps me grounded: think like an engineer, take responsibility like a founder, and stay curious about the people using what we make.
