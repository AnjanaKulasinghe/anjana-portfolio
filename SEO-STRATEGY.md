# Personal Brand SEO Strategy

## Positioning

**Technology Leader · Entrepreneur · Product Creator** is the primary identity. Supporting areas are quality engineering, test automation architecture, software product development and applied AI. Tell the journey from engineering practice and technical leadership into founding a technology company and building products. Use genuine work and product evidence; do not frame the site as a résumé or consultancy sales page.

## Authority, AI discoverability and ecosystem

Maintain crawlable text, canonical/sitemap consistency and accurate Person/Organization relationships. After release, submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, inspect branded query data, canonical selection and indexing. Grow authority through approved, first-hand case studies and legitimate references. Do not add hidden machine-targeted content, artificial links, invented metrics or promises of ranking/AI citations.

Portfolio = personal identity, professional journey and product involvement. KoungaSolutions.co.nz = legal company/trading brand, services and corporate product portfolio. Product sites = product capabilities and customer detail. Cross-reference these properties naturally and keep their copy distinct.
