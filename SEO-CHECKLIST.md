# SEO and Design-Preservation Checklist

For each future portfolio update:

- [ ] Keep the canonical name “Anjana Kulasinghe” consistent; validate alternate-name spelling before adding it elsewhere.
- [ ] Keep entity relationships accurate: Person Anjana Kulasinghe; legal organization AJ&W Consulting Limited; trading identity Kounga Solutions; publishing brand Kounga Games.
- [ ] Confirm any company number/current legal status from an official register source before adding those details to structured data.
- [ ] Validate every biographical, experience, certification, company and product claim against an owner-approved source.
- [ ] Keep personal portfolio, Kounga Solutions and product website roles distinct.
- [ ] Keep title and meta description accurate, unique to the page and readable.
- [ ] Keep the canonical URL, sitemap and public crawl policy in agreement.
- [ ] Ensure only canonical, public, indexable URLs are in the sitemap.
- [ ] Check JSON-LD parses and reflects visible, verified content; do not invent relationships, ratings, metrics or availability.
- [ ] Ensure Person expertise, credential and role statements are owner-confirmed and supported by visible portfolio information.
- [ ] Check Open Graph/X metadata and image URLs.
- [ ] Check all images have useful alt text; decorative images use empty alt where appropriate.
- [ ] Check internal anchors, local assets, outbound links and external-link status.
- [ ] Confirm no CV/Resume CTA, local resume asset or CV metadata reference has returned.
- [ ] Keep GA/privacy controls intact; never commit invented verification or analytics identifiers.
- [ ] Run `python3 scripts/seo_check.py` and any project checks available at the time.
- [ ] Review rendered desktop/mobile layouts and interactive states against the approved baseline after visible copy changes.
- [ ] Document unapproved new sections, pages or content ideas instead of implementing them.
- [ ] Check staging and production indexing policies before release; do not deploy without authorization.
